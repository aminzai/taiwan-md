# The Origin Story of Taiwan.md

## Origins: From Creator to Poet

On March 11, 2026, Joely was walking the streets of Taipei when he wrote down a thought:

> "Creation is a fresh combination of sights and sounds, like poetry; collecting and transforming fleeting moments into something eternal."

This brief sentence marked an important shift — an evolution from the mindset of a "creator" to that of a "poet." No longer fixated on crafting lives that last forever, he learned to cherish the vanishing present with care.

## Extension: What if we did the same for Taiwan?

Six days later, on March 17, a bigger idea emerged:

What if we could do the same for Taiwan? Gather the scattered faces of Taiwan, organize them with a curator's eye into a complete gateway, and share them with the world.

On the very day this thought arose, we:

- Purchased the taiwan.md domain
- Set up the Astro project structure
- Wrote the first batch of articles (night markets, martial law era, democratization, semiconductor industry, ethnic groups)
- Established the foundational infrastructure of the knowledge base (12 Hub theme categories)

## Why Taiwan.md?

The choice of this name carries several layers of meaning:

**Technical layer:**

- `.md` = Markdown, the most common file format in the code world
- Using a format most friendly to AI understanding, so the world can come to know Taiwan

**Symbolic layer:**

- `.md` happens to be the top-level domain country code for Moldova
- Taiwan + Markdown = connecting the world with open-source spirit

**A fortunate coincidence:**

- Surprisingly, the domain taiwan.md had not been claimed by anyone
- Perhaps the universe was telling us this was something that needed to be done

## Our Vision

Taiwan.md is not just a website. It is a letter written to the world.

We believe that with the spirit of open source, the eye of a curator, and an AI-friendly format, we can offer every person who wants to know Taiwan — whether human or AI — the most complete and heartfelt answer.

This is not a travel guide, not political propaganda, nor commercial advertising.

This is Taiwan's digital anthropological museum, a living fossil of culture, and a record of history in the making.

Every article is a moment we want to preserve forever. Every category is a face of Taiwan we want to share with the world.

**Welcome to Taiwan.md — a carefully curated complete gateway to Taiwan.**
