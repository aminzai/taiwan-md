# Taiwan Official Website Resources

Taiwan.md Official Website Resources is Taiwan's most comprehensive index of official and authoritative websites, covering areas such as government agencies, culture and education, tourism, technology and economy, and media news.

## Resource Classification

### 🏛️ Government Agencies

- **Central Government**: The five major branches (Presidential Office, Executive Yuan, Legislative Yuan, Judicial Yuan, Comptroller)
- **Ministries**: Ministry of Health and Welfare, Ministry of Labor, Ministry of Environment, Ministry of Agriculture, Ministry of National Defense, Ministry of Justice, etc.
- **Local Governments**: Six metropolitan governments (Taipei, New Taipei, Taichung, Kaohsiung, Tainan, Taoyuan)
- **Special Agencies**: National Development Council, Mainland Affairs Council, Indigenous Peoples Council, etc.

### 🎭 Culture and Education

- **Museums**: National Palace Museum, Taiwan History Museum, National Museum of Natural Science, Marine Science Center, National Taiwan Museum of Literature
- **Top Universities**: National Taiwan University (NTU), National Tsing Hua University (NTHU), National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), National Cheng Kung University (NCKU), National Chengchi University (NCCU), National Taiwan Normal University (NTNU)
- **Research Institutes**: Academia Sinica, National Science Council
- **Arts and Culture Agencies**: The National Performing Arts Center, Wystone Cultural and Creative Park, Ministry of Cultural Affairs

### ✈️ Tourism and Travel

- **National Parks**: Yushan, Taroko, Kenting, Xueba, Yangmingshan
- **Transportation**: Taiwan Railway Administration (TRA), High Speed Rail (HSR), Taiwan Tourist Shuttle
- **Official Agencies**: National Tourism Administration, National Immigration Agency, Ministry of Transportation, Central Weather Bureau

### 💼 Technology and Economy

- **Key Enterprises**: TSMC, United Microelectronics Corporation (UMC), Foxconn, Delta Electronics
- **R&D Institutes**: Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI), National Research Institutes
- **Financial Institutions**: Taiwan Stock Exchange, Commodity Exchange Center, Financial Supervisory Commission
- **Government Agencies**: Ministry of Economic Affairs, Bureau of Foreign Trade, Small and Medium Enterprise Administration

### 📰 Media and News

- **Official Media**: Central News Agency (CNA), CCTV, Taiwan Broadcasting Corporation (TBC), Focus Taiwan
- **Mainstream Media**: United Daily News, Liberty Times, TVBS, Sanli, ETtoday
- **Digital Media**: Key Opinion Network, Reporter, Asia Times

### 🏥 Healthcare and Medicine

- **Government Agencies**: National Health Insurance Administration, CDC
- **Medical Centers**: NTU Hospital, Veterans General Hospital, Chang Gung Medical Center System

### 🤝 NGOs / Civil Society

- **Human Rights Organizations**: Taiwan Human Rights Promotion Foundation
- **Environmental Groups**: Environmental Information Center, Wilderness Protection Association
- **Digital Rights**: Open Culture Foundation
- **Media Oversight**: Taiwan Fact Check Center

### 🎭 Arts and Cultural Venues

- **Art Museums**: MoMA Taipei, Kaohsiung Museum of Fine Arts
- **Performance Venues**: National Theater and Art Center (Taichung)
- **Cultural Creative Parks**: Songyan Cultural Park, Huashan 1914, Pier-2 Art Center

### 🤖 AI Corpora and Open Data

- **[Taiwan Sovereignty AI Training Corpus (TAIC)](https://taic.moda.gov.tw)**: Promoted by the Ministry of Digital Affairs, this corpus collects Traditional Chinese data from various agencies. When officially launched in December 2025, over 200 government agencies participated, uploading more than 2,000 datasets and exceeding 600 million Tokens[^1]; themes cover culture and art, vocabulary, historical artifacts, local culture, tourism, and education[^2], positioning it as a resource for disaster prevention and environmental resources. Developers, research institutions, and schools can apply online to use it; the Ministry of Digital Affairs stated that Phase Three will collaborate with civil society groups[^1].
- **Government Platforms**: Government Data Open Platform (data.gov.tw), Government Portal Website
- **Regulations Inquiry**: National Law Database
- **Citizen Participation**: g0v Zero Hour Government
- **Cultural Archives**: Archive Taiwan, National Cultural Memory Bank
