> **30-Second Overview:** Media and team official records often introduce this Korean member as "Mingo / Park Min-seo." She entered the lineup as part of the Wing Stars' foreign player strategy following the cheer squad's formation, joining members like An Ji-hyeon to form the team's high-impact public performance roster.

After establishing the "Wing Stars" brand for its cheer squad, the TSG Hung-Ying organization has gradually transitioned the concept of "foreign players" from a one-off topic into an operational structural component. According to reports from media outlets such as _ETtoday Sports Cloud_ regarding the formation press conference, the club announced the addition of the Korean player "Mingo" Park Min-seo; reports also mentioned team structures and supporting measures like training camps in South Korea, indicating that this is not merely about individual exposure, but a complete system of performance and training processes. ## Position Within the TSG System

Unlike the model of "merely maintaining presence on social media," the Wing Stars link the club's home stadium management, training, and public appearance rhythms. Within this structure, Park Min-seo occupies a foreign player position capable of being placed on the press conference stage and sustaining amplified media attention: she is required not only to perform but also to understand the expectations and scrutiny of Taiwanese fans regarding "Korean-style cheering."
